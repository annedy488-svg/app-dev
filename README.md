# 🎬 *Tangled* (2010)

**_Tangled_** is a 2010 animated musical fantasy film produced by **Walt Disney Animation Studios**.  
It is inspired by the classic **Brothers Grimm** fairy tale *“Rapunzel.”*

---

## 🌟 Main Characters
- **Rapunzel** – A princess with magical long hair that can heal and glow.
- **Flynn Rider (Eugene Fitzherbert)** – A witty thief who helps Rapunzel escape her tower.
- **Mother Gothel** – The villain who kidnaps Rapunzel to use her hair’s power.
- **Pascal** – Rapunzel’s loyal chameleon friend.
- **Maximus** – A royal horse who becomes Flynn’s unlikely ally.

---

## 💡 Plot Summary
Rapunzel lives her entire life locked in a hidden tower by Mother Gothel.  
When the thief Flynn Rider accidentally discovers her, Rapunzel takes the chance to explore the outside world for the first time.  
Together, they go on a journey full of adventure, freedom, and self-discovery.
